# 敌人 3D 试点：阶段 1 设计方案

2026-11-20 用户选定：**尸鬼 C（残盔长矛）**、**冰霜精魂 C（冰锥元素）**。没选上的 A、B 方案已删除。

图片由内置 ImageGen 生成；实机样张是对截图的生成式编辑，不是 Godot 实际运行截图，敌人偏大、视角偏正面，只用来看造型方向。实际 3D 身高、0.3 m 离地、绑定和游戏镜头下的辨识度要在后续阶段实机验证。

## 选定方案

| 方案 | 设计要点 | 3D / 动作难点 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 尸鬼 C | 破披风、残盔与长矛形成三角形和斜线，像守墓卫兵。 | 双手持矛需双手 IK 或动作逐帧校正；披风需分骨或布料，防腿部穿插；长矛会影响攻击范围辨识。 |
| 冰霜精魂 C | 放射冰锥肩与胸口冷光，远距离轮廓锐利。 | 尖刺可能遮挡角色或攻击信号；硬片需避开关节变形，雪雾与冷光还需单独处理。立绘双手指形有重叠，三视图要重新画清五指。 |

## 实际使用的完整英文提示词

### 尸鬼 C：立绘

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Create one original full-body enemy character concept portrait for the game Azure Mythic Fantasy. Three-quarter side view, dynamic but balanced stance, entire silhouette and any weapon in frame, portrait orientation, plain solid cool gray background. Realistic anatomy and sculptural proportions, illustrated materials, muted cold palette of azure blue, bone white, deep black and small accents of antique gold. Grounded historic surfaces, clear large shapes; avoid cartoon, thick outline, excess ornament, existing characters, text, watermark. Exactly two arms and anatomically plausible hands, five fingers per visible hand. a heavy-set barrow guardian undead warrior, broad triangular silhouette from a torn short dark cloak, broken crested helmet, holding one long spear firmly with both hands, dark weathered armor. Two legs and two feet clearly grounded, visibly heavier and broader than an ordinary swordsman.
```

### 尸鬼 C：实机样张

输入图 1：参考图/实机近景.png；输入图 2：本方案立绘.png。

```text
Use case: compositing. Image 1 is the exact game screenshot edit target. Image 2 is the enemy character design reference. Add exactly one instance of the character from Image 2 on the open paving immediately to the left of the existing swordsman in Image 1, at the same depth, facing toward him, without overlap. Preserve the screenshot's existing swordsman, scenery, camera, framing, lighting and existing interface exactly; add no new text, labels, UI, or watermark. Recreate the referenced enemy's distinctive silhouette, outfit, colors and weapon accurately as a game-ready 3D character seen from the same elevated oblique game camera as the swordsman. Match the swordsman's low-saturation ink-like two-level light and shade, subtly darkened edges, and scene lighting, not glossy concept art. The enemy has two grounded legs and two arms; its head-to-foot body height is approximately 1.15 times the swordsman's visible head-to-foot height, broad and heavy, with any weapon securely held. Maintain correct hand anatomy, readable silhouette, no extra limbs.
```
