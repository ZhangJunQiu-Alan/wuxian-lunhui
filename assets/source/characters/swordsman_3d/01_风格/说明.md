# 剑士 3D 试点：阶段 1 风格方向

工具：内置 ImageGen 生成立绘及编辑截图；Python Pillow 拼接对比图（中文字体为系统 STHeiti Medium）。这是概念样张，并非 Godot 运行截图或实际 3D 模型。

## 尺寸与局限

原图 1280 × 720，目测原剑士发髻至脚底约 75 像素，目标约 112 像素。样张由 ImageGen 输出为 1672 × 941，A、B、C 人物发髻至脚底分别目测约 126、150、160 像素，折算为 720 高度约 96、115、122 像素；分别约为原人物的 1.28、1.53、1.63 倍。姿势和披肩遮挡使测量有误差。

ImageGen 重绘了整张截图：构图大致保留，但石纹、建筑细节和界面文字发生变化，不能视为原背景像素不变。A、B 有挥刀动作，C 选择了副刀较易辨认的版本，但动作更接近持刀戒备。该尺度下手指和左右手持刀关系无法可靠核验；C 的副刀与地面相近，辨识尤其弱。三张样张用于比较视觉方向，不能证明实机渲染效果或替代用户验收。

## 方向与评价

- **A 画意写实**：厚涂、可信布料和钢铁，冷灰蓝配琥珀暖光。立绘质感和庭院协调最好；样张的冷灰披肩与石面有一定融合，角色略小，双刀要留意对比度。
- **B 英雄造型**：加宽肩背、简化明暗，冷紫暗部与边缘光。样张双刀展开，浅披肩与深色躯干分离较清晰；色彩比背景更紫，图形化语言与场景有轻微差异。
- **C 墨意东方**：干笔、墨晕和少量朱砂，立绘东方韵味最鲜明。缩到样张尺寸后笔触和朱砂细节大多消失，副刀与背景容易混合；灰白色调和雪石协调，但水墨语言在远景不明显。

**推荐 A。** 它最贴合现有美术规范，材质和冷暖关系与庭院比较统一。实际制作时需强化披肩、两把刀的亮度分离，同时测试缩小后的剪影。若把远距离双刀识别作为首要目标，B 的样张更有优势。

## 完整提示词

以下保留实际使用的英文提示词。立绘使用「共用段 + 各方向段」；截图重试使用「截图编辑段」中对应的风格文字；A、B 最终再使用动作修正段。首次截图尝试角色过大，未选用。

### 立绘共用段

```text
Original adult East Asian ronin swordsman, mature athletic realistic human anatomy, black hair tied in a high topknot, exactly two arms and two hands, exactly two distinct blades: long tachi in his right hand, shorter wakizashi in his left hand. Worn pale ash-grey haori shoulder mantle over old dark indigo layers, a modest fur collar, wrapped lower legs and straw sandals bound with cloth for snow. Recognizable silhouette from topknot, pale mantle and two swords. Full body, 3/4 side view, dynamic grounded fighting stance, entire body and both blades visible, portrait vertical composition, simple neutral backdrop. Historical fantasy and original design, no resemblance to existing copyrighted game characters. No text, no logo, no heavy black outlines, no toy-like cartoon, no additional limbs or weapons.
```

### A 立绘追加段

```text
A: painterly realism, 7.5 heads tall, credible heavy cloth, aged leather and steel; thick impasto oil paint strokes, cold blue-grey palette with restrained amber firelight.
```

### B 立绘追加段

```text
B: heroic graphic shape language, about 7 heads tall with broad shoulders and slightly smaller head, striking sharp clean shapes; simplify lighting into two large light and dark value masses, cool violet shadows, restrained warm or cool rim lighting, credible materials.
```

### C 立绘追加段

```text
C: Eastern ink painting fused with Nordic cold colors, dry brush broken strokes and ink washes in the hems, sword motion and hair, preserve dimensional skin and steel; restrained vermilion accents, realistic proportions around 7.5 heads.
```

- C `{style}`：`expressive ink washes and dry brush with subtle vermilion`

### A、B 动作修正段

以各自截图编辑重试结果为输入；C 同样尝试但副刀被遮挡，故未采用修正版。

```text
Edit only the tiny central ronin: put him mid-swing, twisting his torso, right hand swinging the long sword outward while left hand visibly holds the shorter blade apart; both blades unambiguously visible. Keep character exactly the same small size and position, pale mantle, topknot, style, proportions and high oblique camera. Preserve every other part of the image unchanged including all text, floor, buildings and the same framing. No other people, no extra blades or arms.
```
